# Research Idea: BSiC₂ - Unexplored 74K Ambient-Pressure Superconductor

**For submission to: Global Idea Bank, Experiment.com, Materials science forums**

---

## Executive Summary

A hydrogen-free compound (BSiC₂) was predicted in 2020 to superconduct at 74K at ambient pressure via conventional BCS mechanism. Despite this being potentially revolutionary - above liquid nitrogen temperature with no pressure apparatus needed - **zero experimental synthesis attempts have been published in 5 years**.

This represents a significant research gap worth addressing.

---

## The Opportunity

**Material**: BSiC₂ (boron-silicon-carbon compound)

**Predicted Properties**:
- Critical temperature: **74K** (above liquid nitrogen at 77K)
- Pressure: **Ambient** (no diamond anvil cell needed)
- Mechanism: Conventional BCS (electron-phonon coupling)
- Hydrogen: **None** (no stability/handling issues)
- Doping: **None required** (stoichiometric)

**If confirmed**: This would be the highest-Tc hydrogen-free conventional superconductor at ambient pressure ever discovered.

---

## Why It's Underexplored

### Literature Search Results (November 2025)
- Original prediction: Guo et al., New J. Phys. 22 (2020) 076002
- Experimental synthesis attempts: **NONE FOUND**
- Time since prediction: **5 years**

### Why the Gap?
1. Research attention focused on high-pressure hydrides (LaH₁₀, H₃S)
2. Synthesis may be challenging (metastable phase)
3. The prediction received less visibility than hydride results
4. No clear synthesis pathway suggested in original paper

---

## Technical Details

**Crystal Structure**:
- Hexagonal, derived from 2H-SiC
- Si layers alternately substituted with B layers
- Lattice parameters: a = 2.89 Å, c/a = 1.66

**Electron-Phonon Coupling**:
- λ = 2.41 (exceptionally strong)
- E₂g mode shows coupling of λ_qν = 28.96 at specific q-points
- Key feature: Dramatically softened phonon frequencies

**Why It Should Work**:
- Strong coupling from softened phonons (similar to MgB₂)
- No exotic mechanism required - standard BCS
- Related compound BC₃ also predicted at ~40K

---

## Synthesis Challenges

**Known Issues**:
1. Specific BSiC₂ stoichiometry required (not B₁₂(B,C,Si)₃ which forms naturally)
2. Likely metastable - may require non-equilibrium processing
3. Boron solubility in SiC is only ~0.2 wt% under normal conditions
4. No known precursor compounds

**Potential Approaches**:
1. **MBE/CVD**: Atomic layer deposition (B-C-Si-C-B-C...)
2. **High-P/High-T**: Force B substitution under extreme conditions, then quench
3. **Spark Plasma Sintering**: Rapid access to metastable phases
4. **Ion Implantation**: Heavy B implantation into SiC substrates

---

## Recommended First Steps

### Computational (Low Cost)
1. Calculate convex hull energy - how metastable is BSiC₂?
2. Identify kinetic barriers for B→Si substitution
3. Model thin-film stabilization possibilities
4. Check if substrate strain could stabilize the phase

### Experimental (Medium Cost)
1. Attempt MBE growth with alternating B and Si sources on graphite
2. Try spark plasma sintering of B₄C + SiC mixtures
3. B ion implantation into SiC followed by annealing
4. CVD with BCl₃ + SiH₄ + CH₄ precursors
