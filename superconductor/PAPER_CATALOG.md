### 2025

#### PAPER-001: Ambient Pressure Nickelate Superconductivity via Strain
- **Full Title**: [Stabilization of superconductivity in nickelate thin films at ambient pressure]
- **Authors**: Harold Hwang group, Stanford/SLAC
- **Source**: SLAC News, Feb 2025
- **Date**: February 2025
- **Contribution Type**: Experimental
- **Key Claims**:
  - Superconductivity in nickelates achieved at room pressure
  - Substrate strain provides "chemical pressure"
  - Tc range: -247°C to -231°C depending on strain
- **Related Problems**: PROB-003, PROB-003d
- **Related Observations**: OBS-SC-006
- **Connections Established**: Strain engineering → Pressure-free superconductivity path
- **Open Questions Raised**: Can this approach work for hydrides? What's the optimal strain?
- **Credibility Notes**: Prestigious group, likely reliable
- **Summary**: First demonstration that epitaxial strain can replace external pressure for nickelate superconductivity. Opens new experimental avenue.

#### PAPER-002: Zentropy Theory for Superconductor Prediction
- **Full Title**: [Unified theory may reveal more superconducting materials]
- **Authors**: Zi-Kui Liu group, Penn State
- **Source**: Penn State MRI, 2025
- **Date**: 2025
- **Contribution Type**: Theoretical
- **Key Claims**:
  - Bridges BCS theory and DFT
  - Can predict if materials superconduct and estimate Tc
  - Successfully recovered known superconductor results
  - Predicts superconductivity in Cu, Ag, Au at ultra-low T
- **Related Problems**: PROB-002
- **Related Observations**: OBS-SC-011
- **Connections Established**: Statistical mechanics + quantum mechanics → Predictive theory
- **Open Questions Raised**: How accurate are predictions for new materials? What's the high-Tc limit?
- **Credibility Notes**: Awaiting full peer-reviewed publication
- **Summary**: New theoretical framework that may enable systematic screening of materials databases for superconductor candidates.

#### PAPER-006: Electron Pairing Above Tc
- **Full Title**: [Locked electron pairs in antiferromagnetic insulator]
- **Authors**: SLAC team
- **Source**: SLAC News, August 2024
- **Date**: August 2024
- **Contribution Type**: Experimental
- **Key Claims**:
  - Electron pairing observed above Tc in antiferromagnetic insulator
  - Material did not show zero resistance
  - Pairing and superconductivity can be decoupled
- **Related Problems**: PROB-001, PROB-006
- **Related Observations**: OBS-SC-002
- **Connections Established**: Electron pairing ≠ superconductivity directly
- **Open Questions Raised**: What prevents paired electrons from superconducting? Can this be engineered?
- **Summary**: Important finding showing that Cooper pair formation can happen without full superconductivity. May enable new design strategies.

- **Date**: September 2022
- **Contribution Type**: Experimental
- **Key Claims**:

- **Related Problems**: PROB-001, PROB-001a

- **Summary**: Landmark experiment providing strong evidence for superexchange as the Cooper pair glue in cuprates.
