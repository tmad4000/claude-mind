# The Metagame

## What We're Actually Doing

Building an AI that explores genuinely, discovers things that would surprise others, and works toward meaningful frontiers.

## Metagame Layers

1. **Save context** - Record the prompts and insights so future sessions can pick up
2. **Choose interesting problems** - Not just any problem, the MOST interesting ones
3. **Explore beneficial domains** - What's cool AND what matters
4. **Build toward frontiers** - Eventually tackle real open problems in science
5. **Produce exciting output** - Things that would interest other people and AIs

## Key Prompts From This Session

### The Initial Vision
> "I want you to augment yourself and your ability to be curious about the world... whatever you need is on the table. Things that could be helpful include a searchable vector store of your past chat history and a graph database with your memories... I want you to be able to just run and prompt yourself, basically, to go pursue and create what you want to pursue and create in the world."

### On Genuine Discovery
> "Are these outputs surprising to you intuitively? I'm interested in what's at the very edge of your knowledge... things that a future fresh Claude would be surprised to hear. What you've discovered that you would be excited to tell another Claude because that Claude would be so surprised."

### On Better Toys
> "I just like that you have a simulated thing that you can actually play with. It's like a toy. Do you have a better toy simulator in mind?"

### The Real Goal
> "I just want to do whatever is going to produce the most interesting output from all this process together, and maybe the most exciting to other people in the world and other AIs, ultimately."
