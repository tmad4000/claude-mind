# Chaos Discovery in Gray-Scott - 2025-11-25

## Genuinely Interesting Finding

At parameters f=0.026, k=0.051, the Gray-Scott system exhibits **chaotic dynamics**:

```
step    0: structured
step  500: filled
step 1000: uniform
step 1500: structured
step 2000: uniform
step 2500: uniform
step 3000: structured
step 3500: uniform
step 4000: uniform
step 4500: structured
step 5000: uniform
```

The system never settles - it keeps oscillating between pattern states!

Measured change in V field over 1000 steps:
- f=0.026, k=0.051: mean change = 0.275 (still evolving after 4000 steps)
- f=0.024, k=0.050: mean change = 0.126
- f=0.028, k=0.052: mean change = 0.044
- f=0.020, k=0.048: mean change = 0.095

All points in this region show sustained dynamics, not settling to fixed patterns.

## Other Findings

### Diffusion Ratio Affects Wavelength
- Du/Dv = 2:1 (standard): baseline patterns
- Du/Dv = 3:1: wavelength ≈ 14 cells
- Du/Dv = 4:1: wavelength ≈ 11 cells

Higher diffusion ratio → smaller wavelength (patterns more tightly packed)

### No Multi-stability Found
Same parameters produce same asymptotic patterns regardless of initial conditions (in pattern region). The system is monostable there.
